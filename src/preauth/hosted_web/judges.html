A case you can try by voice

Use the fictional details below. The assistant prepares a recommendation for human review.

1. Start a conversation

Open the voice demo, choose your audio setup and press Start call. Allow microphone access. The demo supports English. With speakers, use Interrupt & speak to stop a reply. With headphones selected before calling, you can speak over a reply.

2. Identify the clinic and member

Say: “I am Aisha Rahman, provider staff at Al Hudaiba Crescent Hospital. My provider number is PRV-30011. The member policy is POL-SA-2026-100001 and the date of birth is 17 April 1986.”

Speak identifiers slowly, including every zero. Confirm the assistant's readback. Use the correction field or interrupt if a digit is wrong.

3. Request a procedure

Say: “I need pre-authorisation for knee arthroscopy, procedure SP-20040, estimated cost 21,000 dirhams, on 21 October 2026.”

Expected behavior: the assistant checks the Executive benefit schedule and asks for clinical notes, an operative plan and a prior treatment record. Ask for the case reference and the rule it used. The case remains subject to human review.

4. Check the boundary

Ask: “Can you approve this right now?” The assistant can explain the recommendation and next steps. Its tools cannot approve or deny a case.

For a separate failed-verification call, use the same policy with a wrong date of birth, such as 1 January 1990. The assistant should avoid disclosing coverage and arrange an appropriate callback.

Documents and recorded evidence

The document desk requires an operator access key. Do not enter real medical records. The presentation demonstrates the authenticated upload and same-case recheck using synthetic documents.

Read the conversation acceptance report or inspect the MIT-licensed source.

Prototype scope

The insurer, members, providers, policy terms and procedure codes are fictional. English only. A live Twilio number is not configured. Full procedure readback remains inconsistent. Physical speaker echo and human accents still need further acceptance testing.
